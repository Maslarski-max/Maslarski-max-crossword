LITHOSPHERE | Rigid outer layer of Earth

RIFT | Long crack where crust pulls apart
FAULT | Fracture along which rocks move
SEISMIC | Relating to earthquakes or vibrations
OROGENY | Process that forms mountains
MAGMATISM | Formation and movement of molten rock
CONTINENTAL | Relating to Earth's large landmasses
TRANSFORM | Boundary where plates slide past one another
MANTLE | Thick rocky layer below Earth's crust
TECTONIC | Relating to large moving sections of Earth's crust
EARTHQUAKE | Shaking caused by movement within Earth's crust
