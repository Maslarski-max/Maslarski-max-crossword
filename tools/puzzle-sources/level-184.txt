DAGUERREOTYPE | Early photograph made on a silver-coated plate
CALOTYPE | Paper-based photographic process invented by Talbot
NEGATIVE | Image with light and dark tones reversed
EMULSION | Light-sensitive coating on photographic film
APERTURE | Opening that controls light entering a camera
EXPOSURE | Amount of light recorded by a camera
DEVELOPER | Chemical bath that reveals a latent image
FIXER | Solution that makes a developed image permanent
CONTACT | Printing method placing a negative against paper
PLATE | Flat surface used to record an early photograph
LENS | Curved glass that focuses light
SEPIA | Brown tone often seen in old photographs
TRIPOD | Three-legged support for a camera
