APOGEE | Point in an orbit farthest from Earth
PERIGEE | Point in an orbit nearest to Earth
ECCENTRICITY | Measure of how stretched an orbit is
INCLINATION | Tilt of an orbit compared with a reference plane
PRECESSION | Slow change in the direction of an orbit's axis
GRAVITY | Attraction between objects with mass
VELOCITY | Speed together with a direction
ESCAPE | Motion that overcomes a body's gravitational pull
ELLIPSE | Oval curve followed by many orbiting bodies
SATELLITE | Object that travels around a larger body
LAGRANGE | Name for a special point in a two-body system
ORBITAL | Relating to a path around a body
PERTURBATION | Small change to an object's expected motion
