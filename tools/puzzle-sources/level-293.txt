NEURON | Nerve cell that sends electrical signals
SYNAPSE | Junction where nerve cells communicate
AXON | Long projection carrying signals away from a cell body
DENDRITE | Branch that receives signals from other cells
TRANSMITTER | Chemical messenger released at a synapse
REFLEX | Fast automatic response to a stimulus
NETWORK | Interconnected group of nerve cells
IMPULSE | Brief electrical signal in a nerve
PLASTICITY | Ability of a nervous system to change
SENSORY | Relating to information from the senses
MOTOR | Relating to movement
INHIBIT | Reduce or stop an activity
NEURAL | Relating to nerves or the nervous system
