IRRIGATION | Artificial supply of water to crops
BIODIVERSITY | Variety of living things in an area

COVERCROP | Plant grown to protect and enrich soil
EROSION | Wearing away of soil by water or wind
ORGANIC | Produced without most synthetic farm chemicals
NITROGEN | Nutrient needed by plants for growth
FALLOW | Left unplanted for a period
SUSTAINABLE | Able to continue without exhausting resources
