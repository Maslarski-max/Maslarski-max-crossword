Crossword – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Crossword ("the app") is developed by Zandi Lab. This policy explains what data the app handles. The game is fully playable offline.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, board contents, scores, coin balance, daily-streak history and settings are stored locally on your device. They are included in Android's encrypted device backup and device-to-device transfer so you don't lose progress when you change phones. We do not operate servers and never receive this data.

Third-party services

ServicePurposeData processed
Firebase AnalyticsUnderstanding which features are used (e.g. puzzle started/completed, hints used) App-instance ID, device and app information, usage events. No advertising ID or ad signals.
Firebase CrashlyticsDiagnosing crashes Crash traces, device model, OS version, app-instance ID.
Google Play IntegrityDetecting tampered copies of the app (only if enabled) Device and app integrity signals.
Google Play Games ServicesOptional sign-in with your Google Play Games profile We read only your display name to show it on the start screen. While you are signed in, the app sends two totals to Play Games leaderboards: Classic levels solved and Arena wins. Google handles sign-in and leaderboards under Google's Privacy Policy. We store and send no Play Games data to our own servers; the app has none.

Data collected by these services is encrypted in transit and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Delete all local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • To request deletion of analytics or crash data associated with your app instance, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
