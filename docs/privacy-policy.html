Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, board contents, scores, coin balance, daily-streak history and settings are stored locally on your device. They are included in Android's encrypted device backup and device-to-device transfer so you don't lose progress when you change phones. We do not operate servers and never receive this data.

Your choices

  • Change your ad-consent choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete all local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • To request deletion of analytics or crash data associated with your app instance, contact us at the address below.
